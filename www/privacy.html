FinTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026 (added: country in the user list)

In short: Your financial records stay on your phone and in your own Google Drive. Nobody else, including FinTrack's owner, can see them. The only things shared with FinTrack's owner are your name, email address and country, so they know who uses the app.

What FinTrack is

FinTrack is a personal finance app for recording accounts, entries, budgets, EMIs and cheques. It runs on your phone as a web app (iPhone and other browsers) or as an Android app.

Information you enter

Everything you record (accounts, amounts, notes, plans and cheques) is stored on your own device. It is not sent to FinTrack or to any third party.

Google sign-in and the permissions FinTrack asks for

FinTrack asks you to sign in with Google so it can back up your book to your own Google Drive. It requests only these permissions:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile). Used only to show which account is signed in, inside the app on your device.
  • Files that FinTrack creates in your Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). FinTrack creates a folder named FinTrack and saves your backups there. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

The connection goes directly from your device to Google. Your sign-in pass is stored only on your device and expires automatically.

Who-uses-FinTrack list (name, email and country)

When you sign in, and at most once a day while you use FinTrack, the app sends the following to a private Google Sheet owned by FinTrack's owner:

  • your name and email address from your Google account;
  • whether you use the Android app, an iPhone or a browser, and the FinTrack version;
  • the country you chose in FinTrack (suggested from your phone's time zone; you can change it in Manage → Settings). FinTrack never asks for or sends your location, city or GPS position;
  • the date you first signed in and the date you were last active.

To prove the sign-in is genuine, the app sends a Google ID token. It only confirms who you are; it cannot open your Google Drive or your backups. No financial information is ever sent. The list is used only so the owner knows who uses FinTrack and can contact users about the app. It is not sold or shared. Using FinTrack requires this list; to be removed from it, contact the owner (see Contact) and stop using the app.

How your Google data is used

  • Backups are written to the FinTrack folder in your Google Drive when you press Save, and read back when you sign in on a new device or choose to restore.
  • FinTrack does not sell, share, transfer or disclose your Google data or your financial records to anyone. The only exception is your name, email and country in the owner's user list described above.
  • FinTrack does not use your data for advertising, profiling or training of any kind.
  • FinTrack's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Files saved on your phone

On Android, FinTrack also saves Excel backups in Documents/FinTrack/Backups on your phone. You can also download statements and backups yourself. These files stay on your device under your control.

Analytics, cookies and tracking

FinTrack contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. Apart from the user list above, it stores your data and settings in your device's local app storage and your own Google Drive.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: Manage → Data → Clear everything, or delete the app (or the website's data).
  • In Google Drive: delete the FinTrack folder.
  • Remove FinTrack's access: visit myaccount.google.com/connections and remove FinTrack.

Children

FinTrack is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on FinTrack's Google sign-in screen.
